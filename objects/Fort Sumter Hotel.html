    Transcript: Charleston's Only Waterfront Hotel THE FORT SUMTER HOTEL Charleston, South Carolina On the Battery EAST Typical Floor Plan THE FORT SUMTER IS A COLONIAL HOTEL OTHER COLONIAL HOTELS ARE: MIAMI COLONIAL Miami, Florida WILLIAM PENN HOTEL Miami Beach, Florida COLONIAL ORANGE COURT Orlando, Florida PLAZA APARTMENT HOTEL Houston, Texas THE FORT SUMTER is Charleston's outstanding hotel. Spacious lobbies, sun parlors and terraces, comfortably and luxuriously furnished, overlook the water and offer cordial hospitality in an atmosphere to be found in few hotels. 'THE two hundred outside guest rooms, each with combination tub and shower, appeal to the most fastidious. The comfortable beds are equipped with famous Spring Air mattresses, the finest obtainable. ALTHOUGH located in the city's most exclusive residential section the Fort Sumter Hotel is less than 10 minutes distance from the main shopping and theatre district and is immediately adjacent to the financial center and to the old and historic part of the city. THE cruisine of the Fort Sumter Terrace Dining Room is under the expert direction of a famous chef. The choicest fresh seafoods and noted Southern dishes are featured here in Charleston's foremost dining place. hows clearly the hotel Just beyond the boats in this picture is Castle Pinckrrey and in the distant center background is famous Pore Sumter.
